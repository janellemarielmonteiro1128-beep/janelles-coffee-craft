Caramel Macchiato (Warehouse Coffee)

Our signature Caramel Macchiato is crafted with freshly pulled espresso shots layered over velvety steamed milk and sweet vanilla syrup. Finished with a decadent crisscross drizzle of rich butter caramel sauce, this classic beverage offers a perfect harmony of bold coffee flavor and smooth caramel sweetness.
