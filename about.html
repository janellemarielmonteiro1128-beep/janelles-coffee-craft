About Our Shop

Janelle's Coffee Craft was born out of a genuine passion for artisanal brews and cozy cafe cultures across Metro Manila. What started as late-night study sessions fueled by iced lattes grew into a dedicated mission to curate and craft exceptional espresso drinks.

We believe every cup tells a story—from carefully sourcing rich coffee beans to perfecting the balance of sweetness and roast intensity in every drink we serve.
